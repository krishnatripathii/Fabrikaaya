What is Fabrikaaya
Fabric sourcing
was broken.

The Indian textile industry runs on $150 billion in transactions — and most of it still happens through intermediaries, phone calls, and physical visits to wholesale markets. Designers waste weeks finding the right fabric. Suppliers struggle to reach buyers beyond their geography.

Fabrikaaya is the platform that changes all of this. A curated, technology-first marketplace where quality fabrics meet the people who need them — efficiently, transparently, and beautifully.
